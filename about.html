About us

Our story

Picky Pantry started as a university project by a small team who were tired of staring into the fridge. We believe a good meal shouldn't need a shopping trip, so we turn the ingredients you already own into dinner.

How it works

  1. Type the ingredients you have and press Enter.
  2. Optionally narrow things down by diet, cooking time or difficulty.
  3. Recipes are ranked by how many of their ingredients you already have.
  4. Open a recipe to see what you're missing, the steps, and similar ideas.

What we care about

Less food waste, quick weeknight meals, and recipes that respect vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free diets.
